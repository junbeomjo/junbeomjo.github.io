Assistant Professor

Junbeom Jo

조준범

Jeonbuk National University · Department of Civil Engineering

Junbeom Jo is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at Jeonbuk National University. His research examines the physical processes and cascading impacts of coastal hazards, including typhoons, waves, storm surges, and compound flooding. By integrating numerical modeling, hydraulic experiments, and socioeconomic risk assessment, he aims to advance science-based strategies for climate adaptation and resilient coastal communities.

Research interests

Linking coastal processes, risks, and resilience

01

Coastal Hazard Modeling

We simulate typhoons, waves, storm surges, and tides to understand and predict coastal hazards.

02

Compound Flooding

We examine interactions among storm surge, waves, rainfall-runoff, drainage systems, and coastal defenses.

03

Computational & Experimental Hydraulics

We combine numerical analysis and hydraulic experiments to investigate complex coastal and urban flows.

04

Climate Risk & Resilience

We assess socioeconomic impacts and explore pathways for climate adaptation and coastal resilience.
