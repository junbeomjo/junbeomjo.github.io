Research fit

Who may be a good fit

We welcome students from civil, coastal, ocean, hydraulic, and environmental engineering, as well as related computational and data fields. Curiosity, initiative, and a willingness to learn across disciplines matter most.

Coastal Engineering Hydraulic Engineering Climate Adaptation Numerical Modeling Hydraulic Experiments Risk & Data Analysis
03

Application process

How to inquire

  1. 01

    Prepare your materials

    Prepare a CV, academic transcript, and a brief note about your research interests.

  2. 02

    Send an email

    Introduce yourself, the program or opportunity you are considering, and your preferred start period.

  3. 03

    Discuss the research fit

    Selected applicants will be invited to discuss interests, possible topics, and next steps.

Admissions & funding

Admission follows Jeonbuk National University requirements. Funding opportunities may be available depending on the position and project, and details will be discussed individually.

Domestic and international inquiries are welcome.
